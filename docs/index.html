Playback

A lightweight video editor for macOS, made for simple editing.

Download for Mac

Timeline

Any clip on any track, with gaps allowed. Clips snap to edges, the playhead and keyframes.

⌘T splits, ⌘Delete closes the gap, ⌘G groups.

Canvas and keyframes

Move, resize, rotate and crop clips on the picture itself. The diamond next to a property adds a keyframe; position keyframes draw a path you can drag.

Callouts, arrows and blur

Drag text, callouts, arrows, blur, pixelate, highlight or spotlight onto the canvas. Text works with any installed font, emoji included.

Zoom and pan

Draw a frame on the canvas with the zoom tool to zoom there at the playhead. Text and annotations stay sharp when zoomed.

Effects

14 effects to drop on a clip, from a colour grade with ready-made looks to drop shadow, border and chroma key. Stack them, reorder them or switch them off.

Transitions

24 transitions, from cross-dissolve to wipes, slides and circle reveals. Drop one on a cut or a clip edge; the timeline keeps its length.

Sound

Every clip with sound shows its waveform and a volume line. Double-click the line to add a point, drag the clip's corners to fade.

Export

MP4, WebM, GIF, M4A, MP3, WAV or a PNG of the current frame, from 120p to 4K, with YouTube, vertical and square presets.

Keyboard shortcuts

Every key can be changed in Settings.

Space
Play or pause
J K L
Play backwards, stop, play forwards
⌘E
Export
⌘⇧F
Preview mode

File formats

Video
MP4, M4V, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI, FLV, MPEG-TS, MTS, 3GP, up to 8K
Sound
MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV, AIFF, FLAC, OGG, Opus
Images
JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, APNG

Requirements

System
macOS 15 or later
Chip

Disk
